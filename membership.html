Data checks in Insightly · records the membership team may want to tidy

    How these numbers work. Everything comes from the Last Membership Year field on Organisations in Insightly, read every 6 hours. Only totals are stored; no member names or contact details leave Insightly. An organisation is a member of a year when its last membership year is that year or later. Organisations with 2050 are counted as lifetime or complimentary members and are left out of the renewal rate. New means the AIAC Joined Date is in this year; returning means status Returning after a gap; everyone else renewed. Not renewed is last year's members whose last year is still last year. Dues are the Dues Amount before tax. Insightly keeps only each member's latest payment, so the dues chart covers the current renewal cycle, and history builds up from October 2026 onward.
