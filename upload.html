5. Event attendance

Enter how many people actually attended each event. PheedLoop only counts people checked in at the door, so for events without check-in (like CAIC) type the number here. Leave a box empty to use PheedLoop's check-ins.

6. Monthly sentiment

Enter the month's positive, neutral and negative mentions from your social listening or scheduling tool. Counts or percentages both work. The dashboard turns them into a net sentiment score from −100 (all negative) to +100 (all positive): positive minus negative, as a share of all mentions.

7. Notes on the charts

Add a short note to a month, such as “CAIC early-bird launch” or “Boosted LinkedIn post”. It appears as a dot on that month in the dashboard charts, and the text shows when someone hovers over it, so a spike or dip explains itself. One note per month for each group of charts.

8. Monthly brief

When you save a month's numbers, Claude writes a short brief for Emily from everything on the dashboard. The draft is emailed to you. Read it here, edit anything, then Approve & send to email it to Emily from your Outlook. The approved brief also appears at the top of the dashboard for that month.

10. Facebook & Instagram connection

Connect AIA Canada's Facebook Pages once and the dashboard reads their statistics every morning, along with any Instagram business account linked to them: followers, views, engagements, page views, link taps and posts. It is read-only: the dashboard can't post or change anything. Sign in with a Facebook account that has full control of the Pages, and tick every Page you want counted. Once connected, the Facebook and Instagram rows in the monthly template are no longer needed, except Facebook link clicks, which Meta no longer reports for a whole Page.
